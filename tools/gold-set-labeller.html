The question

Would someone who has started this title, but not finished it, learn a specific plot event they would rather discover themselves?

Spoiler

  • Deaths, survivals, serious injuries
  • Hidden identities and allegiances
  • Twists, or that a twist happens at a given point
  • How it ends, who wins or loses
  • Strong hints at a specific event

Not a spoiler

  • Opinions on acting, music, visuals
  • The premise, up to the end of episode 1
  • Cast, dates, filming facts
  • Vague reactions: "that ending made me cry"
  • Questions and meta comments

Specific beats vague. Judge this sentence with only the one before it. Full rules: docs/labelling-guideline.md.
